# robot-peanutbutterandjelly

## Asumptions
-The bread is out
-The peanut butter is out and the lid is off
-The jelly is out and the lid is off
-The knife is next to the bread, peanut buter, and the jelly

## Instructions
-Grab peanut butter with left hand and toss it up behind your back over your right should and catch it with your right hand 
-Do a litle dance and get a litle excited because you are about to make a peanut buter and jelly
-Grab knife with left hand and put it into jar of peanut buter and get a smooth even amount of peanut butter onto knife and set down jar of peanut butter
-grab one slice of bread with right hand and use knife to evenly spread peanut buter onto one side of bread
-set down bread, pick up jelly, and use knife in the jar of jelly to get an even amount onto the knife and then set down jar of jelly
-Pick up the other piece of bread with right hand, evenly spread jelly onto one side, and then set down the knife
-Grab piece of bread with the peanut butter on it and put the peanut butter side against the jelly side of the other piece

## Done When
-Lid is put on peanut butter jar and jar is put back in pantry
-Lid is put back on jelly and jelly is put back in refridgerator
-Bread is closed up with bread tie and put back in pantry
-Knife is rinsed and placed into the kitchen sink
-Sandwhich is eaten
-Plate is put into the sink
-Crumbs are cleaned up and put into trash
